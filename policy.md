## P1 Prohibited items
Counterfeit, replica, fake or knock-off goods are prohibited. Listings for weapons, prescription drugs and adult content are not allowed on this marketplace.
## P2 Misleading claims
Do not claim health cures, miracle results or guaranteed outcomes. Misleading health claims and guarantees must be removed.
## P3 Unverifiable claims
Superlatives such as best in the world, number one, or 100% authentic need evidence. Authenticity, genuine and original claims need proof; unverifiable claims must be removed or backed by evidence.
## P4 Title style
Titles must be at most 80 characters, use normal capitalization, and avoid repeated exclamation marks or ALL CAPS shouting.
## P5 Clear descriptions
Describe specific details such as material, size, and performance. Avoid vague words like nice, amazing or good quality; vague wording must be replaced with specific details.
## P6 Required attributes
Each category requires attributes: Electronics need brand and condition; Clothing needs size and condition; Home needs condition and material; Books need condition and author; Services need duration. Missing attributes make a listing incomplete.
## P7 Pricing
Price must be a positive number with at most two decimals. No "contact for price".
